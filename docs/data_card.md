# Data Card: Financial Fraud Detection Engine (Synthetic Scenarios)

## 1. Dataset Summary & Provenance

- **Dataset Identifier**: `data/scenarios.parquet`
- **Total Transactions**: 6,367,603
- **Simulation Time Span**: 744 simulation steps (representing 31 calendar days at 1 hour/step).
- **Domain**: High-throughput mobile money and banking transactions (PaySim base schema: `TRANSFER`, `CASH_OUT`, `PAYMENT`, `CASH_IN`, `DEBIT`).
- **Target Variable**: `label` (Binary: `0 = Legitimate`, `1 = Fraudulent`).
- **Synthetic Generator**: Deterministic scenario generator (`fraudengine/data/scenario_generator.py`) with fixed PRNG seeds (`seed=42`).

---

## 2. Synthetic Data Statement & Limitations

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Synthetic Nature Notice**:
> This dataset is synthesized for academic and benchmark research. It builds upon statistical distributions derived from the public PaySim mobile money financial log benchmark, augmented with 10 structured financial crime typology sub-generators.
>
> **Implications of Synthetic Data**:
> 1. **Zero Customer PII**: All account identifiers (`orig_account`, `dest_account`) are pseudonymous synthetic tokens generated by hashed sequences or deterministic formats. No real customer data, account numbers, or banking credentials exist.
> 2. **Controlled Base Rate**: The legitimate transaction background rate is statistically controlled (~99.8% legitimate, ~0.2% fraud), mimicking realistic class imbalance without production banking noise.
> 3. **Behavioral Stationarity**: While 10 distinct fraud patterns and temporal periodicity (diurnal cycles) are explicitly encoded, real-world macroeconomic disruptions, black-swan events, and novel unmodelled criminal behaviors may present different statistical signatures.

## 3. Fraud Typology Scenarios (10 Benchmark Classes)

The synthetic dataset injects 10 rigorous financial fraud topologies designed to test different layers of the defense stack:

| Scenario ID | Name | Typology Description | Primary Detection Vectors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S01** | Account Takeover (ATO) | Credential stuffing followed by sudden password/device change and rapid balance draining | `is_new_device`, `hour_deviation`, `orig_drained`, `amount_zscore` |
| **S02** | Rapid Exhaustion | High-velocity burst draining an account in multiple rapid transfers within minutes | `count_1h`, `sum_1h`, `amount_vs_sum_1h`, Rule `R02` |
| **S03** | Smurfing / Structuring | High-frequency transfers just below regulatory reporting thresholds ($9,500-$9,900) | `count_24h`, `distinct_payees_1h`, Rule `R10` |
| **S04** | Dispersal / Mule Fan | Single high-value source dispersing funds across 10-50 fresh mule accounts | Graph out-degree, `distinct_payees_24h`, entropy |
| **S05** | Collection / Inflow Tree | Multiple distinct compromised accounts funneling funds into a single aggregator account | Graph in-degree, `payee_fan_in_24h`, in-degree spike |
| **S06** | Cyclic Laundering | Closed transaction loop ($A \to B \to C \to A$) designed to disguise origin of illicit funds | Directed cycle detection (`detect_cycles_directed`), PageRank |
| **S07** | Pass-Through Mule | Account receiving funds and immediately transferring 98-100% out within 15 minutes | `payee_pass_through_flag`, `payee_outflow_inflow_ratio_24h` |
| **S08** | Dormant Account Revival | Account inactive for >180 days suddenly initiating large transfers | `dormancy_days`, `time_since_last_txn`, Rule `R08` |
| **S09** | Device/IP Velocity Farm | Single synthetic device ID or subnet orchestrating fraud across dozens of accounts | `accounts_per_device`, `ip_prefix_sharing_count`, Rule `R05` |
| **S10** | Slow-and-Low Evasion | Deliberately spaced transfers spaced over multiple days to evade hourly velocity caps | Behavioural z-score, `fraud_exposure_2hop`, IsolationForest |

## 4. Train / Validation / Test Temporal Splits

To prevent temporal leakage and realistic evaluation:
- **Train Window**: Steps 1 to 496 (Days 1 to 20) — Used for feature store warm-up, out-of-fold anomaly fitting, and XGBoost training.
- **Validation Window**: Steps 497 to 620 (Days 21 to 25) — Used for Optuna hyperparameter tuning, Isotonic probability calibration, and threshold setting.
- **Test Window**: Steps 621 to 744 (Days 26 to 31) — Strictly held-out test split for Phase 5 benchmark evaluation.
